# ECommerce-Funnel-Analysis
## Overview
This project performs an end-to-end customer analytics on a real 
dataset from a UK based online gift store covering transactions 
between 2009 and 2011. The goal was to identify where the business 
is losing customers and provide actionable recommendations to 
improve retention and revenue.

## Dataset
- Source: Online Retail II Dataset (UCI / Kaggle)
- Business: UK Based Online Gift Store
- Period: 2009 — 2011
- Size: 1 Million+ transactions
- Customers: 5,878 unique customers
- Countries: 38 countries

## What This Project Does
- Data Cleaning and Processing
- Purchase Funnel Analysis
- Cohort Retention Analysis
- RFM Customer Segmentation
- Strategic Business Recommendations

## Key Findings
- 27.6% of customers never made a second purchase
- 35% of the entire customer base is completely Lost
- Lost and At-Risk segments together make up 50% of all customers
- Champions and Lost segments are almost equal in size — a major warning sign for the business

## Recommendations
- Send win-back campaigns to the 35% Lost customer segment
- Introduce a VIP programme to protect Champion customers
- Use post-purchase email sequence to fix the 27.6% drop-off
- Shift marketing budget from acquisition to retention
- Target Promising customers with bundle offers to increase frequency
